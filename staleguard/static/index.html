    Is this answer still true?

    Pick a sample or paste an answer, then press Check freshness.

    01ExtractSplits the answer into atomic claims and flags the time-sensitive ones: prices, versions, laws, people in roles.
    02Re-verifySearches the live web for each claim and checks it against fresh, dated sources.
    03Score & fixGives each claim a 0–100 freshness score and rewrites the answer with corrections and citations.

    How the freshness score works

    Every claim gets four components. You can see them under each claim card.

    Prior driftHow likely the fact has changed before looking at evidence: 1 − 0.5^(age / half-life). Half-lives: pricing 120 days, versions 180, company info 240, statistics 365, regulation 540.
    RecencyHow fresh the newest relevant source is (1.0 = this month, 0.5 ≈ six months old).
    AgreementShare of decisive sources that agree with the verdict.
    ConfidenceHow sure the judge is. Low confidence leans on the prior.

    Verdicts: current (sources confirm) · outdated (newer sources show a different value) · partly current · unverified (nothing conclusive, scored from the prior) · timeless (e.g. who founded a company, not checked).

    Answer score = 0.6 × mean + 0.4 × lowest claim. One wrong price makes an answer unsafe to act on, so a single stale claim pulls the whole score down. Grades: Fresh ≥ 85 · Mostly fresh ≥ 65 · Needs review ≥ 40 · Stale < 40.
