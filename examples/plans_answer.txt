OpenAI (ChatGPT)

Free: Launched with GPT-3.5. Later tiers gave free users GPT-4o access, which OpenAI restricted to paid subscribers in August 2025.
Plus ($20/month): Launched in February 2023 and remains the main paid tier. It has included GPT-4, GPT-4o, and later GPT-5 access.
Pro ($200/month): Introduced in late 2024 with unlimited access to o1, the reasoning model that was then the company's strongest. OpenAI later added a $100 Pro tier with lower usage limits.
Team and Enterprise: Business tiers for organizations, with pricing that has been more stable than the consumer plans.

Flagship models: GPT-3 (API, 2020), GPT-4 (March 2023), GPT-4o (May 2024), o1 (December 2024), and GPT-5 (August 2025).

Google (Gemini)

Free: Gemini app access with limited usage, using the models current at each point.
Google One AI Premium / Gemini Advanced ($19.99/month): Launched in February 2024 as Google's first paid Gemini tier, giving access to its top model.
Google AI Pro ($19.99/month): Replaced the Gemini Advanced branding at Google I/O in May 2025, adding 2 TB of storage and higher limits.
Google AI Ultra ($249.99/month): Also launched in May 2025 for power users, with exclusive access to the Deep Think reasoning mode and the Veo 3 video model.

Flagship models: Gemini 1.0 (December 2023), Gemini 1.5 Pro (2024), Gemini 2.0 (December 2024), and Gemini 2.5 Pro (March 2025), which became the default top model on Pro and Ultra plans.

Overall trend: Both companies settled on a similar structure: a free tier, a roughly $20 mid tier, and a premium tier above $200 for heavy users. Pricing and model names changed often, so check each company's current pricing page for exact figures.
